## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A girl at a sunlit table, chin on her hands, looking up at Halo. In front of her a plate of flatbread and salad, a clay cup, and a bowl of grapes and fruit; shelves of pots behind her and an open window onto green hills. The caption reads, “Sometimes we wonder who takes care of us and gives us what we need.” The girl asks, “Who takes care of us?” and Halo asks back, “Who do you think provides you with everything you need?”
2. The same table. A thought cloud opens above her: a bearded man in a red-brown cloak walking with a staff beside a rocky brook, a black raven on a stone behind him. The girl asks, “Is it God?” and “How does He help?” Halo answers, “Let’s see how God helped his prophet Elijah!”
3. Samaria. Elijah stands on the palace steps with his staff and one hand open, facing King Ahab in a gold crown and a purple and crimson robe; soldiers with spears and a red banner with a gold lion stand behind them, and the walled city rises on the hill beyond. The caption reads, “Elijah was a prophet of God who lived in Samaria. At that time, King Ahab was ruling the land.” Elijah says, “God has told me that it will not rain in this land anymore, unless I say so.”
4. The land dried out. Cracked earth runs to the horizon between withered corn, with a stone well and a toppled jar in the middle distance and people gathering what little there is — a woman with a basket, a child beside her, another woman carrying a pot on her head. Elijah stands at the left with his staff under a white sun. The caption reads, “As God had said, it did not rain. Everyone suffered without rain. There was drought and famine in the land.”
5. The brook. Elijah kneels on the rocks and drinks from his cupped hands, water running bright between the stones with green leaves overhanging and red cliffs beyond. Two captions: “But God prepared a way to protect Prophet Elijah from hunger and thirst,” and “God told Elijah to go and hide near the Brook Kerith. There he could drink water from the brook.”
6. Two ravens come down out of the trees, one carrying a round loaf and the other a piece of meat. Elijah sits on the rocks with both hands raised to take it, the brook running past below. Captions: “But what would Elijah do when he was hungry? God prepared ravens for that,” and “They brought bread and meat to the prophet in the morning and in the evening.” Elijah says, “God has provided for me!”
7. The same place, gone quiet. The brook is a thin trickle between cracked stones now, and Elijah sits back against the tree with his staff beside him, looking down at it. The caption reads, “The prophet stayed comfortably near the brook, until the water ran out.”
8. Back at the table, and it is full now — a basket of bread, a bowl of soup, apples and grapes, a jug and a cup. The girl smiles up at Halo and says, “God takes care of me too!” Halo answers, “Yes! We can be thankful for all He provides.” The caption reads, “God took care of Elijah. He provides for us too,” and a banner along the bottom reads, “I will be grateful to God who provides me with food, clothing and shelter.”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 07 “THE PROPHET AND RAVENS”, pp.36–37 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS” and “MARK IT AS RIGHT OR WRONG”*

**1. Where did the prophet Elijah live?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name Samaria as the city Elijah lived in.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “Once upon a time, there lived a prophet named Elijah in the city of Samaria.” (p.36)

**2. What was the name of the King who never obeyed God?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name Ahab as the king who never obeyed God.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Ahab. “King Ahab was ruling Samaria at that time. King Ahab never obeyed God.” (p.36)

**3. Where did God tell the prophet to go when there was drought and famine?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name the brook of Kerith as the place God sent him to hide.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “God told his prophet to go and hide near brook of Kerith.” (p.36)

**4. How did God take care of his prophet there?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know both halves of the care: water from the brook, and ravens bringing bread and meat morning and evening.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “There, the prophet could drink water from the brook… God prepared ravens for that. They brought bread and meat to the prophet in the morning and in the evening.” (p.36)

**5. When Ahab was the king of Samaria, there was famine and drought.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is Right — the drought came while Ahab was king.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Right. “As God had said, it did not rain… Also, there was drought and famine in the land.” (p.36)

**6. God told the prophet Elijah to go to a mountain.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is Wrong — God sent him to the brook of Kerith.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Wrong. “God told his prophet to go and hide near brook of Kerith.” (p.36)

**7. The ravens brought bread and meat to the prophet, in the morning and evening.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is Right — bread and meat, twice a day.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Right. “They brought bread and meat to the prophet in the morning and in the evening.” (p.36)

**8. God nourishes and cares for the creatures of nature.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is Right — the book prints it as a statement to be marked Right.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Right, as the book has it.
