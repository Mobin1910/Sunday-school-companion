## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. Halo floats beside a boy at his desk at home and asks, “Have you ever gone to church with your family?”
2. The boy looks up from his Bible at his desk and Halo glows beside him. Halo asks, “Did you know something special happened when Jesus was a baby?” and then, “Let’s go back to the temple in Jerusalem…”
3. Mary carries the baby and walks beside Joseph up the sunlit temple steps, past two white doves. The words read, “Mary and Joseph took baby Jesus to the temple in Jerusalem.”
4. An old man with a long white beard leans on his staff among the temple pillars, looking upward. The words read, “There was an old man named Simeon who was waiting for the Savior. God’s Spirit had told Simeon that he would see Him.”
5. Simeon reaches out toward the baby in Mary’s arms as Joseph looks on. The words read, “Then Simeon saw baby Jesus. He knew this was the Savior.”
6. Simeon waiting among the temple pillars, while Halo asks a question about him.
7. Simeon holds the baby close and looks up, beaming. The words read, “Simeon took baby Jesus in his arms and praised God,” and Simeon says, “My eyes have seen your salvation!”
8. Mary presses her hands together in wonder and Joseph smiles as Simeon holds the baby. The words read, “Mary and Joseph were amazed at what Simeon said. They praised God for all they had seen.”
9. A crowd around Simeon raises their hands, delighted. The words read, “Everyone who heard Simeon was amazed, and they praised God.”
10. Joseph lifts a hand in praise beside Mary and the baby, and Simeon laughs with them. The words read, “Mary and Joseph praised God too!”
11. Mary holds the baby with her eyes closed in thanks, Joseph and Simeon beside her. The words read, “They thanked God for His wonderful plan.”
12. Mary rides a donkey with the baby while Joseph walks beside her, on a road home at sunset. The words read, “Then Mary, Joseph and baby Jesus went back home.”
13. Back at the boy’s desk at home, Halo glows beside him. Halo says, “Jesus was brought to the temple when He was a baby,” and asks, “Do you know what happened when you were a baby?”
14. The boy turns to his parents at the table, and Halo says, “Ask your parents about your baptism,” and, “They can tell you about that special day.”
15. The family look at a photo album together while Halo asks, “Do you know who your Godfather or Godmother is?” and, “Ask your parents!”
16. The boy holds his hands to his chest and says, “I want to live for Jesus!” Halo answers, “And you can grow in His love every day,” and the words below read, “Your family can help you grow closer to God.”
