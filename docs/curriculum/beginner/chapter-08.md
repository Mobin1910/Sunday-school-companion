## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A boy at a sunlit table building a small wooden boat, loose strips of wood all around him and an instruction sheet open beside it. His mother leans in and points at the sheet; Halo glows at his shoulder. The caption reads, “The boy was building a small boat, but he was not sure why he had to follow the instructions.” The boy asks, “But Amma, why do I have to do it that way?” and his mother answers, “Because I want you to follow the instructions first.”
2. The same table, his mother working in the background now. The boy rests his chin on his fist, holding a strip of wood, and looks up at Halo. The caption reads, “The boy was still wondering. He wanted to know why he had to follow the instructions.” He asks, “But what if I don’t understand why?” and Halo asks back, “Would you still follow the instructions?”
3. A big illustrated Bible lies open on the table, showing the ark on one page and a rainbow on the other. The caption reads, “The boy thought about Halo’s question. Had anyone in the Bible ever faced something like this?” The boy asks, “Has that ever happened in the Bible?” Halo answers, “Yes! Someone in the Bible had a very big instruction to follow,” and the boy asks, “Who?”
4. A crowded town in the sunshine, and it has gone wrong: men arguing with their hands up, one seizing a boy by the arm, others watching from a rooftop. Noah stands apart at the front with his staff, looking up. The caption reads, “Long ago, people had increased on the earth. But they had turned away from God and sinned.”
5. Noah sitting on a low wall outside his house, light breaking through the cloud above him and the valley beyond. The caption reads, “But God was kind to Noah. He told Noah about the flood and gave him an important command.” Out of the light come the words, “Noah, I am going to send a great flood,” and “Build an ark.” Noah answers, “I will listen, Lord.”
6. The ark half-built on its scaffolding, workers up on the frame and at the saw-benches. Noah carries a long beam on his shoulder, coming towards us. The caption reads, “Noah obeyed God. He began to build a great ark out of gopher wood.” Noah says, “God has asked me to build an ark. I will obey.”
7. The ark finished, huge against a blue sky, its ramp down. Noah stands with his hands on his hips looking up at it, his family behind him. The caption reads, “Noah built the ark just as God had commanded him.” Noah says, “I have done what God asked me to do.”
8. The animals coming. Elephants, zebras, giraffes, lions, sheep, deer and two rabbits move up the ramp and in at the door, birds flying in above them. Noah points and his family watch. The caption reads, “Then God told Noah to bring the animals into the ark, two of each kind, male and female.” Noah says, “Look! They are coming to the ark,” and the girl beside him says, “Just as God said!”
9. Seen from inside the doorway, looking out. Noah stands in the opening with his staff and one arm out in welcome; his family and the animals come up the ramp behind them, the green hills beyond. The caption reads, “Noah, his family, and the animals entered the ark, just as God had commanded.” Noah says, “Come, everyone. It is time to enter the ark.”
10. Night, and the rain coming down hard. The ark’s windows are lit and the water is rising over the rocks against its side. Noah and his family look out of an upper window. The caption reads, “Then the rain began to fall. It rained for forty days and forty nights.” From the window comes, “God will keep us safe.”
11. The ark alone on a dark flooded sea, waves breaking against it and broken timber in the water, mountaintops just showing through the rain. Its windows are warm and lit. The caption reads, “The waters rose, and the earth was covered by the flood. But Noah, his family, and the animals were safe in the ark.”
12. Inside the ark by lamplight. Noah sits on the straw with his family; lions, sheep, rabbits, deer, elephants and giraffes rest all around them and two parrots and two doves sit up on the beams. Rain runs down the window behind. The caption reads, “But Noah, his family, and all the animals were safe inside the ark.” Noah says, “God has kept us safe.”
13. The great door open on a green valley in the morning, and everyone coming out — Noah first with his staff, his family behind, then the elephants, giraffes, zebras, lions and sheep down the slope. The caption reads, “After the flood, the waters went down, and Noah and his family came out of the ark.” Noah says, “The flood is over. God has brought us through.”
14. A rainbow across the whole sky over the valley, the ark small on the hill behind and the animals spread out over the grass. Noah and his family stand together looking up. The caption reads, “God made a promise to Noah: there would not be another flood to destroy the earth. He placed a rainbow in the cloud as a sign of His promise.” Noah says, “God has kept His promise.”
15. Back in the boy’s bedroom, his half-built boat on the desk. He rests his chin on his fist and thinks. The caption reads, “The boy remembered the question he had asked at the beginning.” Halo asks, “Remember what you wondered?” The boy says, “Would I still obey if I didn’t understand why?” and Halo asks, “What do you think Noah’s story teaches us?”
16. A thought cloud opens above the desk showing Noah up on the ark’s frame with a mallet in his hand. The caption reads, “The boy thought about Noah. Noah obeyed God, even when he did not know what would happen next.” The boy says, “Noah obeyed God even when he didn’t know what would happen,” Halo answers, “Yes. He trusted God’s command,” and the boy says, “I can do that too!”
17. The boy fitting the little house onto the deck of his finished boat, smiling. The caption reads, “The boy made his decision. He would obey God’s commands, just as Noah did.” He says, “I will obey God’s command, just as Noah did,” and Halo answers, “I’ll be with you as you learn and grow.”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 08 “ARK OF LOVE”, p.40 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Who did God command to build the ark?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name Noah as the one God commanded.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Noah. “But God was kind to the righteous Noah. God commanded Noah to build an ark out of gopher wood.” (p.40)

**2. Which wood was used to make the ark?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name gopher wood as what the ark was built from.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Gopher wood. “God commanded Noah to build an ark out of gopher wood.” (p.40)

**3. The ark had __________ storeys.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the ark was three-storeyed.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Three. “He built a great three-storeyed ark.” (p.40)

**4. Who should enter the ark?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know all of who went in: Noah, his family, and two of every living creature, male and female.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “God asked Noah to get himself and his family into the ark. Similarly, from all the living creatures on earth, he was told to put two each, male and female, into the ark.” (p.40)

## 5. The games this chapter already has

Artwork is reused as game cards, so this matters while you draw:

- a card may letter a **question**, never an **answer** a child is marked on;
- the shelf crops each card to a **centre square** and prints the game's title across the bottom of it, so any lettering must survive that crop whole and sit clear of the title;
- the whole shelf is read before any game is played, so a picture that letters **any** game's answer is barred from **every** card.
