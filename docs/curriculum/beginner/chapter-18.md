## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A boy with a backpack stands at the open door of a church, pointing up at the cross on the gable, families going in ahead of him and Halo glowing beside him. The caption reads, “Have you ever wondered why we go to church? It is a special place where we come together to worship God, learn about Him, and grow together.” Halo asks, “Where do we go to worship God?” and the boy answers, “To church!”
2. Inside the church, stained glass and a wooden cross, a band playing and a woman reading from a Bible at the front, children in the pews with their hands folded. The caption reads, “At church, we come together to worship God, learn His Word, and grow together.” Halo says, “That’s right! Look at all the ways we can be part of God’s house!”, a girl says, “We can pray, sing, listen and learn!”, and Halo asks, “Exactly! And do you know who loved being in God’s house too?”
3. A golden ribbon of light opens out of the church and a sunlit city appears behind it, with a man, a woman and a boy walking in it. The caption reads, “Jesus loved going to God’s house too. When Jesus was twelve years old, something special happened in Jerusalem.” The boy asks, “Jesus?”, Halo answers, “Yes! Jesus.” and then, “Let’s go back in time and see what happened.”
4. Mary, Joseph and the boy Jesus walk together through a crowd toward the great gold-fronted temple, banners flying and pilgrims all around them. The caption reads, “Jesus and His family arrived in Jerusalem for the Festival of the Passover. They travelled together with their relatives and friends and spent the festival in Jerusalem.”
5. A long column of travellers winds away down a hillside road with the city on the ridge behind them, Mary and Joseph among them. The caption reads, “After the festival was over, Mary and Joseph began the journey home with their relatives and friends. They thought Jesus was travelling with the group.”
6. Mary has stopped on the road with a hand on Joseph’s arm, both of them looking back. The caption reads, “After travelling for a day, Mary and Joseph realized that Jesus was not with them. They began looking for Him among their relatives and friends.” Mary asks, “Where is Jesus?” and Joseph answers, “Let’s look for Him among our relatives and friends.”
7. The camp at the roadside, tents and loaded donkeys and barrels, Mary turning among the crowd with her hand at her chest while Joseph shades his eyes and searches. The caption is the same as the panel before it, word for word: “After travelling for a day, Mary and Joseph realized that Jesus was not with them. They began looking for Him among their relatives and friends.”
8. Mary and Joseph walking back up the road toward Jerusalem, the walled city ahead of them, other travellers passing the other way. The caption reads, “They could not find Jesus among their relatives and friends. So they went back to Jerusalem to look for Him.” Mary says, “We must go back to Jerusalem.” and Joseph answers, “Yes. We will find Him.”
9. The boy Jesus sits on the temple steps among bearded teachers, one hand raised as he speaks, the men leaning in to listen, and Mary and Joseph coming up the steps behind them. The caption reads, “After three days, they found Him in the Temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking questions.”
10. Mary kneels down to Jesus on the steps with a hand on his shoulder, Joseph behind her. The caption reads, “When His parents saw Him, they were amazed. Mary spoke to Jesus.” Mary says, “Son, why have you treated us like this?” and “Your father and I have been anxiously searching for you.”
11. Jesus sits facing his mother with both hands open as he answers her, the teachers still around them. The caption reads, “Jesus answered His mother.” Jesus asks, “Why were you searching for me?” and “Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?”
12. Mary and Jesus sit together on the step, looking at each other, Joseph standing quietly behind with his hand to his chin. The caption reads, “When they heard this, they did not say anything.”
13. Back in the present, the boy sits in a church pew with a Bible on his lap looking up at Halo, a priest preaching at the lectern and other children in the pews around him. The caption reads, “Jesus loved being in His Father’s house and learning about God. We can also take part in God’s house.” Halo says, “Just like Jesus, we can learn, worship, and take part in church.” and the boy answers, “I can be part of God’s house too!”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 18 “The Boy Jesus at the Temple”, p.84 — “Tell the Answers”*

**1. Where did Jesus’ parents go every year.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Jesus’ parents went to Jerusalem every year.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* To Jerusalem. “Every year Jesus’ parents went to Jerusalem for the Festival of the Passover.” (p.84)

**2. What was the purpose of their journey.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that they went for the Festival of the Passover.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* For the Festival of the Passover. Same sentence, p.84.

**3. At what age was Jesus taken to the festival.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Jesus was twelve years old.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Twelve. “When he was twelve years old, they went up to the festival, according to the custom.” (p.84)

**4. How long did Jesus sit in the Temple among the teachers.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that it was three days before they found him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Three days. “After three days they found him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers…” (p.84) — the lesson gives the three days as the length of the search; see the chapter note.

**5. “Why have you treated us like this.?” Who said to whom?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that his mother said it to Jesus.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* His mother said it to Jesus. “His mother said to him, ‘Son, why have you treated us like this?’” (p.84)

**6. “Why were you searching for me?”. Who said to whom?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Jesus said it to his mother.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Jesus said it to his mother. “‘Why were you searching for me?’ he asked.” (p.84)

## 7. What the build recorded about this chapter

Built from the curriculum photographs in Drive’s Beginner → Chapter 18 → “01 - Curriculum Source”: three pages, p.83 (title, prayer, Bible portion), p.84 (the lesson and “Tell the Answers”) and p.86 (My Decision, the song line, the Memory Verse and the Concluding Prayer).

**p.85 is not in Drive.** The folder was paginated to the end and holds exactly three photographs, and the page numbers jump from 84 to 86 — the same shape as Chapter 14, which skipped p.65. By the shape of this book the missing page is the activity page. Nothing here depends on it: all six printed questions are on p.84 and every answer is in the lesson above them. Worth a photograph if it carries something.

**Both of this chapter’s prayers are not where you would expect.** p.83’s opening prayer is printed in full; p.86’s Concluding Prayer is the stage direction “(Let one child pray. )”. So `prayer` carries the **opening** prayer, as in Chapter 15 and unlike Chapters 12, 14 and 17 where it was the other way round. And there is no `song`: p.86’s “Learn to sing” says only “(Sing a suitable English song.)”, which is an instruction to the teacher, exactly as in Chapter 17.

**A small mismatch between a question and the lesson, flagged rather than patched.** Printed question 4 asks “How long did Jesus sit in the Temple among the teachers.?”. p.84 says only “After three days they found him in the temple courts” — which is how long the *searching* took, not how long he sat. The answer the book is looking for is three days and that is what the second game teaches, but it asks the question the lesson can actually answer: how long until they found him. An editorial question for the teacher.

The stray full stops before the question marks — “year.?”, “journey.?”, “festival.?”, “teachers.?”, “this.?” — are the book’s and are carried verbatim in `curriculum`. The same stop appears inside the Memory Verse on p.86.

**Artwork.** Thirteen panels and both covers came from Drive’s “04 - Story Artwork”, all fifteen distinct by SHA-256, the folder enumerated by exact filename rather than by paging. The twenty-seven text cards this chapter first shipped with are gone rather than reconciled, which is what happened to Beginner Chapters 5 to 17 and to Primary 1 to 3. Each card is now a picture with `alt` describing the scene and quoting what is lettered on it.

This is the kindest set of panels the class has had for shelf cards: twelve of the thirteen survive the square centre crop with their lettering whole, where Chapter 17 managed three of ten. Only panel 2 is cut. Two panels are barred on content instead — 10 and 11 letter the answers to printed questions 5 and 6 — and all four games still have a picture.

Panels 4 and 5 are 941×1671, one pixel short of the canonical 941×1672. Converted without distortion and nothing resized them.

**Three things in the artwork worth the teacher’s eye.**

1. **Panels 6 and 7 carry the same caption, word for word** — “After travelling for a day, Mary and Joseph realized that Jesus was not with them. They began looking for Him among their relatives and friends.” The scenes differ, the road and then the camp, and both are good; the repetition reads as a duplicated caption rather than a deliberate beat. Carried as drawn and recorded here.

2. **Panel 12 softens the lesson’s last line.** p.84 ends “But they did not understand what he was saying to them”; the panel reads “When they heard this, they did not say anything.” Not understanding and not speaking are different things, and the lesson’s is the harder and more interesting one. The `celebration` and the story still follow the lesson.

3. **Panel 11 letters the Memory Verse exactly** — “Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?”, with “my Father’s house” picked out in colour. It is the only confirmation of the wording outside p.86, and it agrees with what the verse carries, including the plain question mark where p.86 prints “.?”.
