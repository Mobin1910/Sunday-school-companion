## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A boy sits cross-legged on his bedroom rug with a toy truck and building blocks around him, Halo glowing beside him. The boy asks, “Halo, why do we have rules?” and Halo answers, “That’s a good question! Let’s discover why God gave His people commandments.”
2. The boy and his little sister kneel on the floor building a tower out of coloured blocks together, Halo floating above them. Halo says, “Rules can help us do what is good and loving.” and the boy says, “I want to learn God’s rules!”
3. Moses stands at the front of a crowd of his people with his staff, tents pitched behind them and the bare rock of Mount Sinai rising into the sky. The caption reads, “After three months of their journey, the Israelites reached the wilderness near Mount Sinai.”
4. Moses climbs the mountain path alone, his staff raised, looking up, with the whole camp and its tents spread out small on the plain below him. The caption reads, “Moses climbed Mount Sinai to receive God’s instructions.”
5. Near the summit, lightning in dark cloud on one side and a burst of golden light on the other, Moses kneels with his hands open. The caption reads, “God gave Moses instructions for the Israelites to follow in their lives.” The light says, “These are My commandments for My people.” and Moses answers, “Yes, Lord! I will listen and obey.”
6. Moses stands in the light holding two stone tablets, one numbered I to V and the other VI to X in Hebrew lettering. The caption reads, “God gave Moses ten important commandments and wrote them on two stone tablets.” The light says, “These are My commandments for My people. Follow them and live the way I have shown you.”
7. Moses holds both tablets up at the foot of the mountain, the heads of the people in front of him. The caption reads, “Moses came down the mountain and showed the people God’s commandments.” Moses says, “These are God’s commandments. Let us obey Him and live the way He wants us to.”
8. Moses holds one tablet against his chest and opens his other hand to the crowd. The caption reads, “Moses read God’s commandments to the people.” He says, “You shall love God with all your heart. You shall be kind, honest, and treat others with love. Do not steal, do not lie, and always do what is right.”
9. Back in the bedroom, the boy rests his chin on his hand beside a globe and a shelf of books, Halo glowing by him. The caption reads, “God wants us to understand and follow His commandments every day.” The boy asks, “So I should love God and the people around me?” and Halo answers, “Yes! Jesus taught us to love God and our neighbors.”
10. The boy sits at his desk with his eyes closed and his hands folded over an open book, Halo beside him. The caption reads, “God wants us to follow His commandments with devotion. We can show our love for God and our friends every day.” He says, “I will love God!” and “I will love my friends!” Halo answers, “That’s a wonderful decision! When we love God and others, we are following His commandments.”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 17 “The Ten Commandments”, p.79 — “Tell the Answers”*

**1. Where did Israel reach after completing three months journey.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Israel reached the desert near Mount Sinai.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* The desert near Mount Sinai. “After three months of that long journey, they reached the desert near Mount Sinai.” (p.78)

**2. Why did Moses climb up to the mountain.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that God gave Moses instructions and directions on the mountain.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Because God gave him instructions there. “On the mountain God gave Moses instructions and directions which the Israelites were to practice in their life and in their journey.” (p.78)

**3. How many Commandments has God given.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that God gave ten commandments.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Ten. “There were ten important points… They are known as the ten Commandments.” (p.78)

**4. What did Jesus teach about the summary of ten Commandments.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Jesus summed them up as loving God and loving your neighbour.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Two commandments — love God, and love your neighbour. “1. Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your mind and with all your strength. 2. Love your neighbor as you love yourself.” (p.78)

## 7. What the build recorded about this chapter

Built from the curriculum photographs in Drive’s Beginner → Chapter 17 → “01 - Curriculum Source”: four pages, p.77 (title, prayer, Bible portion), p.78 (the lesson), p.79 (“Tell the Answers” and “Fill in the Blanks”) and p.82 (My Decision, the song line, the Memory Verse and the Concluding Prayer).

**p.80 and p.81 are not in Drive.** The folder was paginated to the end and holds exactly four photographs, and the page numbers jump from 79 to 82. Nothing in this chapter depends on them — all four printed questions are on p.79 and every answer is in the lesson on p.78 — but by the shape of this book two pages is more than an activity page, and they may well carry the ten commandments listed out in full, which this chapter summarises rather than lists. Worth a photograph if they do.

**The workbook’s answer and the lesson’s answer disagree on question 2, and the games follow the lesson.** “Why did Moses climb up to the mountain.?” is answered on p.78 by “On the mountain God gave Moses instructions and directions which the Israelites were to practice in their life and in their journey.” But p.79’s “Fill in the Blanks” 2 offers only “to take rest” or “to pray”, and the lesson says neither. The second game uses the lesson’s reason as its correct answer and keeps “To rest from the journey” as a wrong option, so a child working from that page finds it here and finds it wrong. This is an editorial question for the teacher.

**There is no `song`.** p.82’s “Learn to sing” says only “(Sing a suitable English song)” — a stage direction to the teacher rather than a song, in the same way Chapters 12 and 14 printed a stage direction where a prayer goes. There is nothing to carry, so the field is absent rather than filled with the instruction.

`prayer` carries the Concluding Prayer from p.82. The opening prayer on p.77 is printed in full as well, but the schema holds one and the closing one is what the rest of this class carries.

**Artwork.** Ten panels and both covers came from Drive’s “04 - Story Artwork”, all twelve distinct by SHA-256, the folder enumerated by exact filename rather than by paging. The twenty-four text cards this chapter first shipped with are gone rather than reconciled, which is what happened to Beginner Chapters 5 to 16 and to Primary 1 to 3. Each card is now a picture with `alt` describing the scene and quoting what is lettered on it.

Panel 8 is 941×1671, one pixel short of the canonical 941×1672. Converted without distortion and nothing resized it.

**Only three of the ten panels can be shelf cards, so one game has none.** Panels 1, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10 all have a speech bubble cut mid-sentence by the square centre crop — at the size a card actually prints, about 165px, a cut bubble reads as broken text rather than as a picture. Panel 9 crops cleanly but letters “Jesus taught us to love God and our neighbors”, which is the fourth game’s answer, and the shelf is read whole before any game is played, so it is barred from every card and not just that one. The third game goes without, for the reason given in its note.
