## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A boy kneels on his bedroom floor and lifts the edge of his quilt to look under the bed, surrounded by a stuffed dog, a toy car and a lost shoe. Halo floats beside him. The panel asks, “Have you ever lost something that was important to you?”
2. A woman in a red robe sits at a lamplit table, holding one silver coin up between her fingers while nine more lie on a woven mat in front of her. Halo glows at her shoulder. “A woman had ten silver coins that were very precious to her.”
3. The same woman rests her cheek on one hand and counts the coins on the mat with the other. Her smile is gone. “But one day, she counted her coins and found that one was missing.”
4. She stands in her darkened house holding a small clay oil lamp out in front of her, peering into the shadows between the jars. “She lit a lamp and began to search every corner of her house.”
5. Bent low over a straw broom, she sweeps the floor of her house and watches the ground as she goes. “She searched every corner of her house. She swept the whole house and kept looking.”
6. Lying down on the floor, she holds her lamp under the table and looks into the dark beneath it. “She looked under the table, behind the jars and in every corner.”
7. She reaches down and lifts the silver coin off the stone floor, beaming, her lamp still burning in her other hand. “At last, she found the missing coin!”
8. She holds the coin up for her friends and neighbours, who have crowded into the doorway with their hands raised. “She was so happy! She called her friends and neighbours to tell them, ‘I have found my coin!’”
9. Jesus sits under an olive tree telling the story, hands open, to people gathered on the ground around him. Halo rests on the rock at his side. “Jesus told this story to show us how much God loves us. Every person is precious to Him.”
10. Jesus talks with two children at sunset, the town on the hillside behind them, while Halo sits on the rock beside the children. “Just like the woman looked for her lost coin, God always looks for us when we are far away. We can always come back to Him.”
11. Jesus holds a child in a long hug, eyes closed, while friends and neighbours clap around them in the evening light. Halo beams beside them. “God is so happy when we come back to Him! He rejoices because every person is precious.”
12. Jesus stands with three children on a hillside at sunset beside a small wooden sign reading “A New Beginning”. Halo smiles at the children's side. “I belong to God. He knows me. He loves me. He is always with me. I can always come back to Him.”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 03 “The Lost Coin”, p.19 — “Answer the Questions”*

**1. How many coins did the woman have?**  
*What the child must take away:* Recall that the woman had ten coins and kept them like a treasure.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Ten. “A woman had ten drachmas (Silver coin, Greek) with her. She kept it like a treasure.” (p.18)

**2. How many coins were lost?**  
*What the child must take away:* Recall that exactly one of the ten went missing — and that one was enough to send her searching.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* One. “One day, she counted her coins and found only nine of them. One coin was missing.” (p.18)

**3. What did the woman do to find the lost coin?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name what she actually did: lit a lamp, searched every corner, swept the whole house, and kept on searching.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “She lit a lamp and searched every corner of her house. But didn’t get it. Then she swept the whole house and searched… She kept on searching for her lost coin.” (p.18)

**4. What did she do when she got her coin back?**  
*What the child must take away:* Recall that her first act on finding it was to call other people and ask them to be glad with her.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “She called together all her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin that I had lost.’” (p.18)

**5. Who told the parable (story) of lost coin?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is Jesus’ own story, and that He told it to show how much God loves us.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Jesus. “This story was told by Jesus.” (p.18)

**6. What should we do to avoid falling away from God’s love?**  
*What the child must take away:* Understand that we are precious to Jesus, and that it is sin that walks us away from Him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Turn away from sin. “We are precious treasures of the Lord Jesus. When we sin, we walk away from Jesus.” (p.18)

**7. What must be done to come back to Jesus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that coming back is confessing — and that Jesus is already searching, the way the woman searched.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Confess our sins and come back. “Jesus continues to search for us, until we confess our sins and come back.” (p.18)
