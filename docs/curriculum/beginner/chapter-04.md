## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A boy sits at his kitchen table with his chin on his hand and an empty white plate in front of him, a dinosaur mug and a bowl of fruit beside it. Halo floats by the fridge. The boy says, “Halo, I'm hungry!” and Halo asks, “What do you do when you're hungry?”
2. The boy stands with a finger to his chin, smiling up at Halo in front of an open kitchen cupboard. He says, “I would ask my amma.” Halo asks, “And would amma bring you food?”
3. His mother leans in and sets down a plate of rice and curry while the boy presses his hands together and beams. He says, “Thank you, Amma!” Halo says, “Your Amma takes care of you! Isn't that amazing?”
4. The boy looks up at a bare cupboard with empty shelves and asks, “What if there was no food at all?” Halo answers, “That sounds hard! Do you know who we can learn from?” — and a glowing cloud opens beside them showing a long line of people walking into the desert behind a man with a staff.
5. The boy, now wearing a small backpack, stands among the desert rocks and watches a long column of people come down the valley with their bundles and animals, an old man in a red cloak leading with a staff. Halo says, “They have been travelling for many days.” The boy says, “They look tired.”
6. In the camp, a woman tips an empty woven basket towards us while a boy sits with his chin in his hands and a girl leans on her hand beside him. An empty sack and a toppled clay jar lie in the sand. “After many days of travelling, all their food was gone. The children were very hungry and tired.”
7. A crowd presses around Moses between the tents, hands open and empty baskets held up. One man calls, “Give us food!” Moses stands to the right with his staff, listening. “The people were very hungry and began to grumble against Moses.”
8. Away from the camp, Moses kneels on the rocks with his hands clasped and his head bowed, his staff laid on the ground beside him, the sun going down over the mountains and the tents small in the valley below. “Moses moved away from the people and knelt down to pray.”
9. Still kneeling, Moses lifts his face to the sky as light breaks through the clouds over the mountains. “Then God said to Moses, ‘Don't be afraid. I will feed them every day.’”
10. At sunrise the desert floor is covered in small white flakes. A boy crouches and lifts one to his mouth, a girl beside him holds another, and a woman fills her hands while Moses watches from behind. Someone asks, “What is it?” “The next morning, the ground was filled with bread.”
11. Moses sits among the children with a handful of white flakes held out towards them, baskets of it around his feet. He says, “This is manna, God-given bread from heaven.” “Filled with surprise, they asked one another, ‘What is it?’”
12. The sky is orange and birds are coming down over the camp. A boy holds a quail in both hands, a girl reaches for another beside her basket, and a woman gathers more into hers while a cooking fire burns behind. “In the evening, quails would fall on the ground. They would cook those birds.”
13. The whole camp eats together in daylight — children sitting in the manna holding pieces to their mouths, baskets full beside them, meat roasting on a frame over a fire, Moses standing among the tents. “During their journey through the desert, God provided them with bread and meat without fail.”
14. Back at his own table with a full plate in front of him, the boy rests his chin on his hand and thinks, “God gave the Israelites food…” Halo glows beside the fridge and asks, “What do you think that means for you?”
15. The boy sits up with both fists raised and a wide grin, his dinner in front of him and Halo bright at his shoulder. He says, “God takes care of me too!”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 04 “Manna”, p.23 — “Answer the Questions” and “Mark it as Right or Wrong”*

**1. To whom did God give bread and meat from heaven?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the bread and meat were given to the people of Israel, travelling in the desert.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* To His people, the Israelites, on their journey through the desert. “During their journey through the desert, God provided them with bread and meat without fail.” (panel 13)

**2. Whom did God send to deliver his people?**  
*What the child must take away:* Name Moses as the one God sent to lead the people out and through the wilderness.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Moses. He is the one leading the column out of Egypt (panel 4), walking at the head of the people (panel 5), and the one the people come to (panel 7).

**3. Why did people complain?**  
*What the child must take away:* Understand that the complaint had a real cause — the food had run out and the children were hungry.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Their food had run out and they were hungry. “After many days of travelling, all their food was gone. The children were very hungry and tired.” (panel 6)

**4. What did Moses do, at that time?**  
*What the child must take away:* Recall that Moses answered the grumbling by going away and praying, not by arguing back.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* He moved away from the people and knelt down to pray. “Moses moved away from the people and knelt down to pray.” (panel 8)

**5. How did God take care of his people?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know both halves of the provision: bread on the ground in the morning, meat in the evening, every day.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* He sent bread in the morning and meat in the evening. “The next morning, the ground was filled with bread.” (panel 10) and “In the evening, quails would fall on the ground.” (panel 12)

**6. The people of Israel were living as slaves in Egypt.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is true — the journey began because they were being brought out of slavery.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* True. The lesson places them in Egypt — “All those people (Israelites) were on their way from the land of Egypt to their own land, Israel.” (p.22)

**7. God sent Moses to deliver the people.**  
*What the child must take away:* Confirm Moses as God's choice — the same fact as question 2, met a second time.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* True. The same answer as question 2.

**8. There was no problem for the people in their journey through the desert.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is false — the journey was hard and their food ran out.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* False. “After many days of travelling, all their food was gone.” (panel 6)

**9. When people grumbled, Moses prayed.**  
*What the child must take away:* Confirm prayer as what Moses did — the same fact as question 4, met a second time.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* True. “Moses moved away from the people and knelt down to pray.” (panel 8)

**10. God was angry, and thus did not feed them.**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that this is false, and that the opposite is what happened — He fed them every day without fail.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* False. “Don't be afraid. I will feed them every day.” (panel 9), and “God provided them with bread and meat without fail.” (panel 13)
