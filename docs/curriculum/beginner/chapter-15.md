## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. A boy lies on a cushion on the living-room rug with his chin in his hands, grinning up at Halo. Behind him his father reads from a Bible on the sofa while his mother and little sister listen. The boy says, “My family loves being together!” and Halo asks, “Do you pray together, too?”
2. The same room, a big open Bible on the floor in front of the boy now. He says, “Hmm… We don’t always remember to pray together.” Halo answers, “That’s something we can learn about!”
3. A glowing vision unfurls out of the open Bible: a bearded man in a blue robe standing with a staff among his family, with sheep and cattle on the green hills behind. Halo says, “I know a story about a man named Job!” and the boy asks, “Wow! What was he like?”
4. Job stands outside his stone house with all ten of his children crowded around him, every one of them looking up at him, the flocks grazing on the hillside behind. The caption reads, “Job had seven sons and three daughters. He had a very large family!”
5. A long table laid with dishes under lanterns at dusk, Job in the middle of his grown sons and daughters. The caption reads, “Job’s children often came together at each other’s homes for special meals and to be with their father.”
6. Job stands at the head of the table holding up a cup, his children raising theirs back, the table loaded with food and the courtyard lit by lanterns. The caption reads, “Job made a big meal for them. They would eat, celebrate, and be happy together as a family.”
7. The meal is over and the family is making music — one son plays a stringed instrument, a daughter holds a tambourine, the rest clap. The caption reads, “They enjoyed the meal and spent time together. Job was so happy to see his children united and loving one another.”
8. Job kneels with his hands pressed together and his eyes closed, and all around him his children kneel and bow their heads the same way, lanterns burning low beside them. The caption reads, “After the meal, Job prayed for each of his children.”
9. Job stands in a wide green pasture with his shepherd’s crook, one hand resting on the head of an ox, sheep crowding round his feet and camels and a donkey behind him. The caption reads, “Job also took care of many animals. He had sheep, camels, cattle, and donkeys.”
10. Job sits on a low stone wall at sunset with his hand at his chin, watching his grown children talking together across the courtyard. The caption reads, “Job loved his children. He wanted them to do what was right and stay close to God.”
11. Job stands with both arms raised beside a stone altar with a fire burning on it, his children behind him with their hands folded and their eyes closed. The caption reads, “After the feast, Job gathered his children and offered sacrifices to God for them.”
12. Back in the living room, the boy sits cross-legged on the rug looking up at Halo, his family on the sofa behind. The caption reads, “Job loved his children and prayed for them. We can pray for our families, too!” Halo asks, “Who can you pray for in your family?” and the boy answers, “I can pray for Mummy, Daddy, and everyone at home!”
13. The whole family kneels together at the coffee table with their hands folded and their eyes closed, an open Bible in front of them and Halo glowing quietly at the end of the table. The caption reads, “Let us praise God, read the Bible, and pray together as a family!”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Beginners Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 15 “Job”, p.69 — “Tell the Answers”*

**1. Where did Job live ?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Job lived in the land of Uz.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* In the land of Uz. “They lived in the land of Uz.” (p.68)

**2. How many sons did Job have ?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Job had seven sons.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Seven. “He had a very large family consisting of three daughters, seven sons, his wife and many servants.” (p.68)

**3. Why did they gather together in one of their houses?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Job’s children gathered to feast together on special occasions.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* To feast together on special occasions. “That day there would be feasting in that house. They would spend their time eating, drinking, cracking jokes with each other and sharing joy.” (p.68)

**4. Why would Job gather his children around ?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Job was worried his children might have sinned.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Because he was worried that one of them might have done wrong. “Job, who is God fearing, is worried whether any of his children would do any wrong.” (p.68)

**5. What did Job do for his children.?**  
*What the child must take away:* Remember that Job prayed and asked God to forgive his children.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* He prayed for them. “He would gather his children together, and pray to forgive them of any sins that they might have done knowingly or unknowingly.” (p.68)
