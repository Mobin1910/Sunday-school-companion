## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. Outside a church on a bright morning. A boy in a white shirt and a blue backpack walks up the path pointing towards the door, where families are going in; a cross stands on the roof and a stained-glass window fills the arch. Halo glows beside him. The caption reads, “Have you ever wondered why we go to church? It is a special place where we come together to worship God, learn about Him, and grow together.” Halo asks, “Where do we go to worship God?” and the boy answers, “To church!”
2. Inside the church. Children fill the pews, a worship team sings at the front with a guitar, and a woman at the lectern holds an open Bible; a wooden cross, a banner with a dove, and a screen showing a crown above an open book. The boy looks up at Halo from his pew. The caption reads, “At church, we come together to worship God, learn His Word, and grow together.” Halo says, “That’s right! Look at all the ways we can be part of God’s house!” A girl beside him says, “We can pray, sing, listen and learn!” and Halo asks, “Exactly! And do you know who loved being in God’s house too?”
3. Still in the church, with the past opening underneath. The boy asks, “Jesus?” and Halo answers, “Yes! Jesus,” and then, “Let’s go back in time and see what happened.” A ribbon of golden light sweeps down into a second scene along the foot of the panel: Mary, the boy Jesus and Joseph walking together towards Jerusalem, the city on its hill behind them. The caption reads, “Jesus loved going to God’s house too. When Jesus was twelve years old, something special happened in Jerusalem.”
4. Jerusalem at festival time. Market stalls with baskets of grapes and clay pots, red banners with menorahs on them, and crowds climbing the steps of the golden Temple. Mary walks with a woven bag on her shoulder, the boy Jesus between his parents, Joseph with his staff. The caption reads, “Jesus and His family arrived in Jerusalem for the Festival of the Passover. They travelled together with their relatives and friends and spent the festival in Jerusalem.”
5. The road home. A crowd of travellers with bundles and a loaded donkey heads away down a stony path among olive trees; Mary and Joseph walk in the middle of the group, seen from behind, with Jerusalem and the Temple on the hill behind them. The caption reads, “After the festival was over, Mary and Joseph began the journey home with their relatives and friends. They thought Jesus was travelling with the group.”
6. The group has stopped among the trees. Mary turns to a woman beside her, alarmed, and asks, “Where is Jesus?” Joseph, a hand to his head, looks out over the crowd and says, “Let’s look for Him among our relatives and friends.” Travellers sit around them with their bundles. The caption reads, “After travelling for a day, Mary and Joseph realized that Jesus was not with them. They began looking for Him among their relatives and friends.”
7. The searching itself. Mary and Joseph move through the camp among awnings, barrels and a donkey, both scanning the crowd; children and travellers fill the slope around them.
8. Back up the road. Mary and Joseph walk together against the flow of travellers, Jerusalem and the Temple ahead of them on the hill. Mary says, “We must go back to Jerusalem,” and Joseph answers, “Yes. We will find Him.” The caption reads, “They could not find Jesus among their relatives and friends. So they went back to Jerusalem to look for Him.”
9. The Temple courts, between tall columns. The boy Jesus sits on the steps among bearded teachers in turbans, talking with his hands open; scrolls lie on a low table in front of them. Mary and Joseph have just come round a column at the left and stopped. The caption reads, “After three days, they found Him in the Temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking questions.”
10. Mary has reached him and put a hand on his shoulder; Joseph stands behind her and the teachers watch. She says, “Son, why have you treated us like this?” and “Your father and I have been anxiously searching for you.” The caption reads, “When His parents saw Him, they were amazed. Mary spoke to Jesus.”
11. The boy Jesus on the Temple steps, both hands open as he answers. He says, “Why were you searching for me?” and “Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?” — the last words painted in blue. Mary sits facing him and Joseph stands behind her. The caption reads, “Jesus answered His mother.”
12. The three of them quiet on the steps, Mary and Jesus looking at each other and Joseph standing behind with a hand at his chin, scrolls on the tables around them. Two captions: “When they heard this, they did not say anything,” and along the bottom, “Jesus went down to Nazareth with His parents.”
13. Back in church today. The boy sits in a pew in a blue hoodie with an open book on his lap, looking up at Halo; a priest in black vestments preaches from the pulpit under a wooden cross, with children in the pews around him. The caption reads, “Jesus loved being in His Father’s house and learning about God. We can also take part in God’s house.” Halo says, “Just like Jesus, we can learn, worship, and take part in church,” and the boy answers, “I can be part of God’s house too!”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 02 “THE HOUSE OF GOD”, p.14 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Why were Jesus’ parents unaware of His absence on their return journey?**  
*What the child must take away:* They assumed He was with their relatives, and travelled a whole day before they began looking.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “But His parents did not know about it. Thinking that He was with his relatives, they travelled for a day.” (p.14)

**2. Where did the parents go in search of Jesus who was missing?**  
*What the child must take away:* Back to Jerusalem — after looking among their relatives and friends and not finding Him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “Then they began looking for Him among their relatives and friends. Since they did not find Him, they went back to Jerusalem to look for Him.” (p.14)

**3. What did the parents see when they found Jesus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Jesus in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “After three days they found Him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking questions.” (p.14)

**4. What was Jesus’ reply when His parents asked Him about making them anxious?**  
*What the child must take away:* “Why were you searching for me? Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?”  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “He asked her, ‘Why were you searching for me? Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?’” (p.14)

## 5. The games this chapter already has

Artwork is reused as game cards, so this matters while you draw:

- a card may letter a **question**, never an **answer** a child is marked on;
- the shelf crops each card to a **centre square** and prints the game's title across the bottom of it, so any lettering must survive that crop whole and sit clear of the title;
- the whole shelf is read before any game is played, so a picture that letters **any** game's answer is barred from **every** card.
