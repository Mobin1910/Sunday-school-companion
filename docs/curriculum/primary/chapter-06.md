## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 06 “IN GOD’S IMAGE”, p.32 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Whom did God create at last?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that man was created last, on the sixth day, after everything else.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Man. “It was after God created everything else that he created man on the sixth day.” (p.32)

**2. How did God create man?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that God formed man from the dust of the ground and breathed the breath of life into him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* From the dust. “He formed man from the dust of the ground and breathed into His nostrils the breath of life and man became a living being. Then God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it.” (p.32)

**3. Why did God create man in his own image?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that it was so that they may rule over the fish, the birds, the livestock, the wild animals and every creature that moves along the ground.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* To rule over His creatures. “So that they may rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky, over the livestock and all the wild animals, and over all the creatures that move along the ground.” (Genesis 1:26, quoted p.32)

**4. Why did God create man?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that God wants us to be responsible caretakers of all His creation, and that He created man in a special way because He loves us so much.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* To care for creation, and because He loves us. “God wants us to be responsible caretakers of all His creation.” and “Thus God created man in a special way because He loves us so much.” (p.32)
