## 1. The story, beat by beat

*19 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. There lived four friends who were helpful to others.
2. One of their friends was paralyzed.
3. He could not get up from his bed or walk.
4. They heard that Jesus was coming to the nearby town, Capernaum.
5. They had heard about the healing miracles done by Jesus.
6. So they decided to take their friend to Jesus.
7. Jesus was at a house at Capernaum.
8. A big crowd surrounded Him.
9. There was no way to get their friend in front of Jesus.
10. But they did not give up.
11. They went up on the roof above Jesus.
12. They dug through it and made an opening.
13. Then they lowered the mat the man was lying on.
14. They had complete faith that Jesus would heal their friend.
15. Jesus saw their faith.
16. He said, “I tell you, get up, take your mat and go home.”
17. Everyone gathered there was surprised.
18. God honours all the services you do for others.
19. Let us also come to Jesus, and bring others to Jesus.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 13 “FAITH OF FOUR FRIENDS”, p.63 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Who brought the paralytic man to Jesus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that his four friends brought him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Four friends. “There lived four friends who were helpful to others. One of their friends was paralyzed … So they decided to take their friend to Jesus.” (p.62)

**2. In which city was Jesus then?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Jesus was at a house in Capernaum.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Capernaum. “Jesus was at a house at Capernaum.” (p.62)

**3. How was the paralytic being taken to Jesus at Capernaum?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the friends dug an opening in the roof above Jesus and lowered the mat the man was lying on.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Through the roof. “They made an opening in the roof above Jesus by digging through it and then lowered the mat the man was lying on.” (p.62)

**4. What did Jesus tell the paralytic?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Jesus said, “I tell you, get up, take your mat and go home.”  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “I tell you, get up, take your mat and go home.” — “Seeing their faith Jesus said to the paralytic, ‘I tell you, get up, take your mat and go home.’” (p.62) The same words are the chapter’s memory verse.
