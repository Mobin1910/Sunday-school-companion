## 1. The story, beat by beat

*29 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. There was a mighty king in a land called Babylon.
2. His name was Nebuchadnezzar.
3. He invaded the Jewish kingdom and took its treasure.
4. The Jews were captured and taken away as slaves.
5. The king made an image of gold.
6. He placed it where everyone could see it.
7. He ordered everyone in the country to worship it.
8. It was a royal order, and everyone was afraid to disobey.
9. So they all began worshipping the image.
10. But three Jews did not obey the king.
11. Their names were Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego.
12. They lived according to the will of Jehovah.
13. They believed God’s commandment was greater than the king’s command.
14. “You shall have no other gods before me.”
15. When Nebuchadnezzar heard of this, he was furious.
16. The soldiers seized the three men and brought them to the king.
17. The king threatened them.
18. “If you don’t worship the golden image, you will be thrown into the fire.”
19. Do you know what they boldly said?
20. “The God we serve is able to deliver us, and he will deliver us.”
21. The king had them tied up and thrown into the blazing furnace.
22. Then Nebuchadnezzar looked into the fire.
23. He saw four men walking there, not three.
24. Shocked, he had the three men released at once.
25. They had not been burnt at all.
26. An angel of God had walked with them in the fire.
27. The king ordered that no one speak against their God.
28. “For no other god can save in this way.”
29. If we trust God, He will deliver us from all danger.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 15 “GOD WHO STANDS WITH US”, p.71 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. What was King Nebuchadnezzar’s command?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that he ordered everyone in the country to worship the image of gold he had set up.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* To worship the golden image. “He made an image of gold and placed it where everyone could see it. He ordered everyone in that country to worship that image.” (p.70)

**2. Why did King Nebuchadnezzar throw the three young men into the furnace?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that they disregarded his order and would not worship the golden image, because God’s commandment was greater than the king’s.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* They would not worship it. “They believed that the commandment of God ‘You shall have no other gods before me’ was greater than the command of the king. So they disregarded the king’s order.” (p.70)

**3. What did King Nebuchadnezzar see in the fire?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that he saw four men walking in the fire instead of the three he had thrown in.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Four men. “Instead of the three men he had thrown into the furnace, he saw four men walking there.” (p.71)

**4. What order did the king give to the people when he saw that the youth were not burnt by the fire?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that he ordered that anyone who said anything against their God be punished.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* That none speak against their God. “the king ordered that anyone who said anything against the God of Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego be punished ‘for no other god can save in this way.’” (p.71)

**5. Who were the three young men who survived the fire?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that they were Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego. “But three Jews who were there — Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego — did not obey the king.” (p.70)
