## 1. The story, beat by beat

*19 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. A man had a fig tree growing in his vineyard.
2. It was big and beautiful, with lots of branches and leaves.
3. But it had no fruit.
4. For three years the owner came looking for fruit on it.
5. He did not find any.
6. He was disappointed and angry.
7. He told the caretaker to cut the tree down.
8. It was only wasting the space and the soil.
9. The caretaker was sad.
10. He asked the master to keep the tree for one more year.
11. He would dig around it.
12. He would add fertilizers and water it regularly.
13. Perhaps it would bear fruit in the coming year.
14. Or else it could be cut down.
15. God is like the caretaker of the vineyard.
16. God has plans for each one of us.
17. If we cannot fulfil them, He will be disappointed.
18. He is willing to work on us and wait for our fruit.
19. It is because of God’s mercy that we are not destroyed.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 10 “MERCIFUL GOD”, p.50 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Where was the fig tree?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the fig tree was growing in the man’s vineyard.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* In the vineyard. “A man had a fig tree growing in his vineyard.” (p.50) The teacher’s hand writes “in the man’s vineyard” beside the question.

**2. Why did the owner of the vineyard become angry?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that for three years he came looking for fruit and did not find any.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* There was no fruit. “For three years the owner of the vineyard had come looking for fruit on the fig tree but did not find any. Disappointed and angry, he told the caretaker of the vineyard to cut down the tree instead of wasting the space and soil.” (p.50)

**3. How did the fig tree get one more year?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the caretaker was sad and asked the master to keep the tree one more year.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* The caretaker asked for it. “The caretaker was sad and requested the master to keep the tree for one more year.” (p.50)

**4. Who is extending our life to live fruitfully?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that it is God, who works on us and waits for us to bear fruit.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* God. “God is like the caretaker of the vineyard … He is willing to work on us and wait for us to bear fruit for Him. It is because of God’s mercy that we are not destroyed.” (p.50) The teacher’s hand writes “God” beside the question.

**5. What all could the caretaker do for the fig tree to bear fruit by the next year?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that he would dig around it, add fertilizers, and water it regularly.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Three things. “He would dig around it, add fertilizers and water it regularly.” (p.50)
