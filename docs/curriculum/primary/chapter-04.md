## 1. The story, beat by beat

*16 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. Jesus was in a town by Lake Gennesaret.
2. A man covered with leprosy came to Him.
3. In those days there was no treatment for leprosy.
4. Lepers were sent out of the town. They lived as outcasts.
5. People were frightened of them. No one would go near them.
6. Not even their own families came close.
7. But when this man saw Jesus, he fell with his face to the ground.
8. He said, “Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean.”
9. Jesus reached out and touched him.
10. He said, “I am willing. Be clean.”
11. Immediately the leprosy left him.
12. Jesus told him to show himself to the priest,
13. and to offer the sacrifices that Moses had commanded.
14. Jesus holds on to us even when all others turn away.
15. When trouble comes, we can ask God with strong faith.
16. And when we ask for healing, let us remember others who need it too.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 04 “JESUS THE HEALER”, p.23 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. How were lepers treated in olden days?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that lepers were sent out of the town and lived as outcasts, shunned even by family.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* They were sent away. “Lepers were sent out of the town and had to live as outcasts, away from other people… No one would go near them, not even their own family members.” (p.22)

**2. How did Jesus heal the leper?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Jesus reached out and touched him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* He touched him. “Jesus reached out and touched him and said, ‘I am willing. Be clean.’ Immediately the leprosy left him.” (p.22)

**3. What did Jesus say to the leper?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know Jesus’s words as He healed him.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “I am willing. Be clean.” (p.22) He also told him to show himself to the priest and offer the sacrifices Moses had commanded; the words the question is after are the healing ones, which are also this chapter’s memory verse.
