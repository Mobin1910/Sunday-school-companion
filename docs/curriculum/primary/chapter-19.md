## 1. The story, beat by beat

*31 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. Jesus had twelve disciples, and they were always with Him.
2. After Jesus was crucified, the disciples were hiding in fear.
3. The Jews persecuted them.
4. The evil King Herod killed James, the brother of John.
5. He was planning to kill more of the disciples.
6. So they seized Peter also.
7. It was the time of the Passover.
8. They put him in prison until the Passover was over.
9. Four groups of four soldiers each were guarding the prison.
10. Peter was tied with two chains, and slept between two soldiers.
11. The rest stood guard outside.
12. Peter’s friends were constantly praying for him.
13. They had faith that prayer would save him.
14. They prayed without ceasing.
15. Suddenly an angel of the Lord appeared.
16. He struck Peter on the side and woke him up.
17. “Quick, get up!” he said.
18. The chains fell off Peter’s wrists.
19. Peter put on his clothes and sandals and followed the angel.
20. They got out of the jail.
21. They passed through the first gate and the second gate.
22. Then they came to the iron gate leading to the town.
23. None of the guards saw them.
24. That door also opened by itself.
25. Peter walked out into the street.
26. When he had crossed the street, the angel was gone.
27. Then Peter knew it was not a dream but real.
28. God had heard the people praying for Peter without ceasing.
29. He delivered Peter unharmed.
30. As a church we have a duty to pray for others.
31. Pray without ceasing for those in sorrow and suffering.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 19 “THE CHURCH THAT PRAYS”, p.91 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. How many disciples did Jesus have?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Jesus had twelve disciples.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Twelve. “Jesus had twelve disciples. They were always with Jesus.” (p.90)

**2. When was Peter imprisoned?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that it was at the time of the Passover, and that he was held until the Passover was over.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* At the Passover. “As it was the time of the Passover, they put him in prison until the Passover was over.” (p.90)

**3. What did the church do for Peter?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Peter’s friends prayed for him constantly and without ceasing.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* It prayed. “Peter’s friends were constantly praying for him. They had faith that prayer would save him. They prayed without ceasing.” (p.90)

**4. How did Peter escape from the prison?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that an angel woke him, the chains fell off, and they walked out past the guards and through the iron gate, which opened by itself.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* An angel led him out. “Suddenly an angel of the Lord appeared … the chains fell off Peter’s wrists … Passing through the first gate and the second gate, he came to the iron gate leading to the town. None of the guards saw them. That door also opened by itself.” (pp.90–91)
