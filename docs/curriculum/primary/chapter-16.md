## 1. The story, beat by beat

*28 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. Jairus was a ruler of the synagogue.
2. He had a little daughter who was very dear to him.
3. One day she got sick, so sick that she almost died.
4. Greatly dismayed, Jairus ran to Jesus and fell at His feet.
5. “My little daughter is about to die.”
6. “You must lay your hands on her.”
7. Immediately Jesus went out with him.
8. But on the way, people came to Jesus with their sorrows.
9. Jesus was very busy healing many people.
10. By that time someone came from the house of Jairus.
11. His daughter had died.
12. “Don’t trouble the teacher anymore.”
13. They thought it would not matter now, even if Jesus came.
14. But Jesus said, “Don’t be afraid, just believe.”
15. Then Jesus went to the house of Jairus.
16. A great crowd followed Him.
17. Jesus said, “The child is not dead but sleeping.”
18. The crowd around them laughed at Jesus.
19. He put everyone out of the room.
20. Only her father and mother and His disciples stayed.
21. He took her by the hand.
22. He said to her, “Talitha koum!”
23. That means, “Little girl, I say to you, get up!”
24. The girl came back to life, and got up and walked.
25. Nothing is impossible for Jesus.
26. He never forsakes those who fall at His feet and plead.
27. Sometimes the answer is not immediate.
28. Be patient and wait.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 16 “JAIRUS’ DAUGHTER”, p.77 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Who was Jairus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Jairus was a ruler of the synagogue, and that he had a little daughter.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* A ruler of the synagogue. “Jairus was a ruler of the synagogue. He had a little daughter. She was very dear to him.” (p.76)

**2. What did Jairus say to Jesus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that he fell at Jesus’ feet and asked Him to lay His hands on his dying daughter.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “My little daughter is about to die. You must lay your hands on her.” — “Greatly dismayed, he ran to Jesus, fell at His feet, and said …” (p.76)

**3. Why did the crowd mock Jesus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that they laughed because Jesus said the child was not dead but sleeping.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Because of what He said. “Jesus said, ‘The child is not dead but sleeping.’ The crowd around them laughed at Jesus.” (p.76)

**4. How did Jesus raise the girl?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that He took her by the hand and said, “Talitha koum!”  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* By the hand, and a word. “He took her by the hand and said to her, ‘Talitha koum!’ (which means ‘Little girl, I say to you, get up!’).” (p.76) The same words are the chapter’s memory verse.

**5. Do you believe that Jesus is a miracle-working God?**  
*What the child must take away:* Meet what the lesson says about Jesus: nothing is impossible for Him, He never forsakes those who plead, and sometimes the answer is not immediate.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* **This question has no right answer and the app does not give it one.** What the lesson says is: “Nothing is impossible for Jesus. Jesus never forsakes those who fall at His feet and plead. Jesus is a God who works wonders … there is nothing that is not done by faith. Sometimes the answer is not immediate. Be patient and wait.” (p.77)
