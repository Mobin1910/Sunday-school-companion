## 1. The story, beat by beat

*These describe panels that already exist — each says what is drawn and quotes what is lettered on it.*

1. Night in a child’s bedroom. A boy sits up in bed clutching a dark blue blanket patterned with gold stars, a teddy and books on the shelf behind him and a full moon at the window. Halo glows beside him. The boy says, “Halo… I had a nightmare. I’m scared.” Halo answers, “Oh no… That sounds scary!”
2. The same room. The boy has both hands on the blanket and is looking up. He says, “I wish Amma was here.” Halo, floating level with him, says, “Amma and Appa care for you very much.”
3. The boy has brightened, a hand at his chin. He says, “Amma and Appa care for me. Who else cares for me?” and then, “How do I know?” Halo answers, “God does. He cares for you wherever you are.”
4. The stable at Bethlehem, warm with lamplight. Mary sits on the straw with the swaddled baby in her arms and Joseph kneels beside her; a donkey looks over the rail and a lamb lies in the foreground, the town and one bright star beyond. The boy is in the scene now, in his pyjamas, watching. He asks, “Who are they?” and Halo says, “This is Mary, Joseph and baby Jesus.” The caption reads, “Long ago, Joseph, Mary and baby Jesus lived in Bethlehem.”
5. King Herod on his throne in a red and gold hall, a crown on his head and a scroll in one hand, jabbing a finger at a man who stands before him with his head bowed. Soldiers with spears line the wall under a banner with a gold lion on it. Herod says, “Find the child!” The caption reads, “King Herod heard that Jesus had been born. He was afraid that Jesus would become King and take his throne.”
6. Joseph asleep on a low bed, propped on one elbow and looking up, wide awake now. An angel in white leans towards him out of a warm light with one hand open; Mary and the baby sleep on behind. The angel says, “Get up! Take Jesus and Mary and go to Egypt,” and Joseph answers, “I will obey God.” The caption reads, “God knew that Jesus was in danger. So He sent an angel to warn Joseph.”
7. Out on the road at night. Joseph walks ahead with a bag over his shoulder, leading the donkey by its rein; Mary rides with the baby held close. Palms, a lit doorway and the moon behind them. Joseph says, “Come, Mary. We must leave now,” and Mary answers, “Yes, Joseph. Let’s go.” The caption reads, “Joseph obeyed God. That very night, he took Mary and baby Jesus and left for Egypt.”
8. The angel a second time, in the same warm light over Joseph’s bed, Mary and the child asleep behind. The angel says, “Get up! Take Jesus and Mary and go back,” and Joseph answers again, “I will obey God.” The caption reads, “After King Herod died, God sent an angel to Joseph again.”
9. Daylight, and the road home. Joseph walks with his staff and his bag, leading the donkey; Mary rides with the child, who is old enough now to look out at the road. A wooden signpost reads “Nazareth” and the hill town rises behind it among olive trees and flowers. The caption reads, “Joseph, Mary and Jesus returned from Egypt. They went to Nazareth and lived there. God cared for them every step of the way.”
10. Morning in the bedroom, the curtains open and sunlight on the quilt. The boy sits up smiling with Halo beside him; a hand-made poster on the wall reads “GOD IS WITH ME” under a red heart. The boy asks, “So God cared for Jesus and His family?” and Halo answers, “Yes. And God cares for you too.” The caption reads, “God knows when we are afraid. He cares for us.”
11. The boy sits on the edge of his bed in star-patterned pyjamas, holding a Holy Bible against his chest with both arms, a rocket and a stack of books on the bedside table. He says, “When I am scared, I can trust God!” and Halo answers, “Yes! God cares for you, and you can obey Him.” The caption reads, “God cares for me. I will trust and obey Him.”

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 01 “GOD WHO CARES”, p.10 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Why did king Herod want to kill baby Jesus?**  
*What the child must take away:* Herod was afraid that Jesus, born to be King of the Jews, would take away his throne.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “King Herod was afraid that Jesus, who was born to be the King of the Jews, would take away his throne. So he sent his people to find and kill baby Jesus.” (p.10)

**2. To which place did the angel tell Joseph to go?**  
*What the child must take away:* Egypt — and that they went that same night, in complete obedience to God.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Egypt. “The angel told him to flee to Egypt with Mary and baby Jesus. In complete obedience to God, they fled to Egypt that night itself.” (p.10)

**3. When did the family of Jesus come back to Nazareth?**  
*What the child must take away:* After king Herod died, when an angel appeared to Joseph a second time and told him to return.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* “After the death of king Herod, an angel appeared before Joseph and told him to return to Nazareth. So Joseph, Mary and baby Jesus went to Nazareth.” (p.10)

## 5. The games this chapter already has

Artwork is reused as game cards, so this matters while you draw:

- a card may letter a **question**, never an **answer** a child is marked on;
- the shelf crops each card to a **centre square** and prints the game's title across the bottom of it, so any lettering must survive that crop whole and sit clear of the title;
- the whole shelf is read before any game is played, so a picture that letters **any** game's answer is barred from **every** card.

### “Why Herod Was Afraid” · `why-herod-was-afraid` · reveal
*Objective:* Follow the chain the lesson gives: Jesus was born King of the Jews, Herod feared for his throne, and so he sent his men.  
*Shelf picture:* `panel-05`  
*Must not be lettered on any card:*
  - Jesus was born in Bethlehem
  - Born King of the Jews
  - Herod feared for his throne
  - He sent men to kill Him

### “Where the Angel Sent Them” · `where-the-angel-sent-them` · multiple-choice
*Objective:* Name Egypt — the place the angel told Joseph to flee to, and not one of the other three the chapter names.  
*Shelf picture:* `panel-07`  
*Must not be lettered on any card:*
  - Egypt

### “When They Went Home” · `when-they-went-home` · sequence
*Objective:* Put the escape and the return in order, so that "after Herod died" is something the child has placed rather than recited.  
*Shelf picture:* `panel-08`  
*Must not be lettered on any card:*
  - 1. Herod wanted to find Him
  - 2. An angel warned Joseph
  - 3. They left for Egypt
  - 4. The angel came again
  - 5. They went to Nazareth

### “God Who Cares” · `god-who-cares` · true-or-not
*Objective:* Hold the lesson's closing paragraph — God foresees our troubles, He helps us overcome them, and we must obey Him.  
*Shelf picture:* `panel-10`  
*Must not be lettered on any card:*
  - Did God know Herod’s plan? — yes
  - Did Joseph wait until morning? — no
  - Does God see our troubles coming? — yes
  - Does God help us through them? — yes
