## 1. The story, beat by beat

*19 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. One day Jesus told a story about a farmer and his seed.
2. A farmer went out to sow his seed.
3. As he was scattering it, some fell along the path.
4. The birds came and ate it up.
5. Some fell on rocky ground, where there was not much soil.
6. It sprang up quickly.
7. But when the sun came up, the plants withered.
8. They had no root.
9. Other seed fell among thorns.
10. The thorns grew up and choked the plants.
11. Still other seed fell on good soil.
12. There it produced a crop.
13. A hundred, sixty or thirty times what was sown.
14. Jesus ended the story this way.
15. “He who has ears, let them hear.”
16. The seed is God’s Word, and our heart is the ground.
17. A heart made ready lets the Word take root.
18. So let us prepare our hearts prayerfully.
19. Then the Word can grow in us and bear much fruit.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 07 “GOOD SOIL”, p.37 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. What happened to the grains which fell on the road?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the birds came and ate it up.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* The birds ate it. “As he was scattering the seed, some fell along the path, and the birds came and ate it up.” (p.36)

**2. Why didn’t the seeds that fell on the rock grow?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that they sprang up quickly but withered when the sun came up, because they had no root.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* They had no root. “Some fell on rocky ground, where it did not have much soil. It sprang up quickly but when the sun came up, the plants withered because they had no root.” (p.36)

**3. What happened to the grains which fell among thorns?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the thorns grew up and choked the plants.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* The thorns choked them. “Other seed fell among thorns, which grew up and choked the plants.” (p.36)

**4. Where did the farmer get income from?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that the crop came from the seed that fell on good soil — a hundred, sixty or thirty times what was sown.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* From the good soil. “Still other seed fell on good soil, where it produced a crop—a hundred, sixty or thirty times what was sown.” (p.36)

**5. How should we prepare our heart to receive God’s Word?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that we prepare prayerfully: we read God’s Word, understand it, and make it part of our life.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Prayerfully. “We should read God’s Word, understand it and make it part of our life.” — **the teacher’s handwriting on p.37, not printed text.** The word “prayerfully” is written in above the question. The chapter’s own My Decision on p.40 says the same thing in the first person.
