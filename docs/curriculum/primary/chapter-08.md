## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 08 “CANAAN, LAND OF MILK AND HONEY”, p.42 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Why did Israelites move to Egypt?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that there was famine in their land, so they came to Egypt.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Because of famine. “When there was famine in their land they had come to Egypt.” (p.42)

**2. Why did Israelites cry to God?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that a new king made them slaves, was cruel to them and made them work hard, and they suffered extreme poverty.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Because they were slaves. “So the new king made them slaves. He was cruel to them and made them work hard. They suffered extreme poverty. Then the Israelites cried to their God for deliverance.” (p.42)

**3. What is special about Canaan?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that it is a good and spacious land, a land flowing with milk and honey.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Milk and honey. “God promised to take the Israelites to a good and spacious land, a land flowing with milk and honey — Canaan.” (p.42)

**4. Who led the Israelites to Canaan?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that God led them, through Moses, whom He sent to lead the exodus.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* God. “Thus God through Moses saved the Israelites and brought them to Canaan.” (p.42) The teacher’s hand writes the same thing beside the question: “God (through Moses)”.
