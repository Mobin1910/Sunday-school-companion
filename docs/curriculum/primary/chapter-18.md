## 1. The story, beat by beat

*32 beats, the cards the app shows today, in order. This is the narrative a panel sequence has to carry.*

1. God loved the people of Israel very much.
2. But they were often disobedient to Him.
3. They would worship Baal instead of the living God.
4. They began to deny the God who had been with them.
5. There was only one prophet of Jehovah left.
6. It was Elijah.
7. But there were four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal.
8. They abused and mocked Elijah all the time.
9. Elijah asked the people of Israel to gather at Mount Carmel.
10. “Get two bulls for us.”
11. “Let Baal’s prophets choose one and lay it on the wood.”
12. “But they must not set fire to it.”
13. “I will prepare the other bull the same way.”
14. “Then you call on your god, and I will call on the Lord.”
15. “The god who answers by fire — he is God.”
16. Baal’s prophets took their bull and prepared it.
17. They called on Baal from morning until noon.
18. “O Baal, answer us!”
19. But there was no response, and no one answered.
20. Then Elijah repaired the fallen altar of Jehovah.
21. He laid his bull on it, cut into pieces.
22. A moat was made around the altar.
23. Water was poured over the offering and the stream around it.
24. Elijah prayed to the God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel.
25. “Answer me, Lord, so these people will know that you are God.”
26. What a miracle!
27. God suddenly sent a great fire.
28. It consumed the offering, the wood and the soil.
29. Even the water in the stream was drained away.
30. When all the people saw this, they fell down and cried out.
31. “The Lord, he is God! The Lord, he is God!”
32. If God is with us and we believe, victory will be ours.

## 3. The lesson's own questions

*Primary Mar Thoma Vol-1, Chapter 18 “THE LIVING GOD”, p.87 — “ANSWER THE QUESTIONS”*

**1. Who was the only prophet of Jehovah who stood firm when everyone else went after Baal?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Elijah was the only prophet of Jehovah left.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Elijah. “There was only one prophet of Jehovah. It was Elijah.” (p.86)

**2. How many prophets did Baal have?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Baal had 450 prophets.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* 450. “But there were 450 prophets of Baal.” (p.86)

**3. Where did Elijah gather the people of Israel?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that Elijah gathered them at Mount Carmel.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* Mount Carmel. “Elijah asked the people of Israel to gather at Mount Carmel.” (p.86)

**4. Was the prayer of Baal’s prophets answered?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that it was not: they called from morning until noon, and there was no response and no one answered.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* No. “The prophets of Baal … called upon the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, ‘O Baal, answer us!’ But there was no response, and no one answered.” (p.86)

**5. How did God answer Elijah’s prayer?**  
*What the child must take away:* Know that God sent a great fire that consumed the offering, the wood and the soil, and even dried the water in the stream.  
*Answer, quoted from the lesson:* With fire. “God suddenly sent a great fire and consumed Elijah’s burnt offering, wood, and soil. Even the water in the surrounding stream was drained away.” (pp.86–87)
